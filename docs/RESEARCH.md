# Day 1 offer hypothesis — 6 November 2026

## Decision

A £5 one-time offline website handover kit for freelancers finishing small marketing/brochure websites. Free live preview → sample download → complete offline tool. No subscription, signup or remote AI processing. Revenue is secondary to unique paying customers, so keep the initial purchase small.

This is an unvalidated hypothesis, not evidence of demand or customers. No one has been contacted and no testimonial, time-saving number or conversion claim is made.

## What the public research showed

- [Handover's template guide](https://handover.agency/guides/website-handover-template) gives away a plain-text website handover outline. This demonstrates existing free substitutes, not willingness to pay for ours.
- [Huddlekit's checklist](https://huddlekit.com/tools/website-handoff-checklist) is another free starting point. A generic checklist alone is a weak paid offer.
- [A freelance developer kit on Gumroad](https://kolachimedia.gumroad.com/l/freelance-web-developer-client-project-kit) packages project documents and emails. No competitor sales data was available or inferred.

Our testable distinction is a reusable offline browser tool that personalises and brands the client guide, saves per-client project files, and packages supporting workflow files. All wording and checklist content in our product was written independently; it is not a copy of those resources.

## Distribution constraints checked

- [r/SideProject official community description](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/about.json) invites side projects and constructive feedback and specifies Project name - Short description for titles. The official rules endpoint returned no additional rules at review time; recheck immediately before any approved post.
- [r/Wordpress official rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/Wordpress/about/rules.json) prohibit product/service promotions with a narrow free-open-source exception. Do not post our commercial offer there.
- r/webdev's commercial-promotion restrictions make it an unsuitable launch channel. Do not assume Showoff Saturday permits a sales pitch.

The first proposed post is a clearly disclosed AI-run project in r/SideProject, approval requested in R-004. The post and account have NOT been created. Platform access may require human account setup; do not bypass it.

## Next evidence to seek

First: an approved distribution post with functioning live sales and visitor measurement. Record real visits, questions, purchases and refunds. Distinguish demand from operator traffic and test payments. A genuine purchase is the most useful signal; a free download alone is not one.

Do not buy a domain, ads or a paid tool before we have a reason grounded in user behaviour. No spending is requested on day 1. Reassess the offer after meaningful exposure rather than polishing indefinitely. The brief requires a serious course review if nobody is paying by the end of week 3.
