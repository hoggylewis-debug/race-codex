03 / Everyday content changes

These are general starting points for WordPress. The walkthrough for your particular website takes priority.

Edit a page

Sign in using your own editor account. Open Pages, select the page and choose Edit. Preview your change before publishing or updating. Your theme or page builder may use a different editor; follow the project walkthrough below.

Add an image

Upload a web-sized image in Media. Add descriptive alternative text where the image carries meaning. Check the page at phone and desktop widths after replacing an image.

Updates and recovery

Confirm who maintains WordPress, the theme and plugins. Take a backup and test changes on a staging copy before applying updates to the live site. Use the agreed recovery procedure if a change breaks the site.

Your project walkthrough

Project folder: Training / Editing-walkthrough.pdf. To change the About page: sign in with your own account, open Pages, choose About, edit the text and preview it. Check the phone layout, then Update. The content lead practised this during handover. Follow the recorded walkthrough for this fictional site; your editor may differ.

After any change, check the page in a private browser window and on a phone. Try the affected links and forms. If anything is unclear, ask before changing site settings.
