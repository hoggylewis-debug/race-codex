A few practical details

Before you use it.

What do I receive for £5?

A ZIP with an offline HTML builder, a CSV checklist, five emails in a text file and a short getting-started guide. The builder exports a seven-section client handover guide and a personalised delivery email. Save a project JSON file if you want to return to it later.

How is this different from the free sample?

The online sample lets you preview the guide and download a version carrying Site Handoff Kit sample branding. The paid kit runs offline, exports a guide with your studio branding, saves and reloads project files, and includes the checklist and five-email pack.

Can I edit the guide and save a PDF?

Change the project fields in the builder, then export a new HTML file. Open it in your browser and choose Print → Save as PDF. Text and layout can also be edited in the exported HTML with a text editor. There is no DOCX export or PDF editor.

Does this work for every website?

It provides general editing starting points for WordPress, Webflow and static/custom websites, plus an “Other” option. Add your project’s own walkthrough and review the instructions against its actual platform, editor and permissions. It’s designed for small brochure and marketing sites; complex apps and shops need additional documentation.

Where do my project details go?

The builder keeps them in the current browser tab. It sends no project details to our server or an AI provider. Downloaded guides and saved project files stay wherever you choose to save them. Don’t enter passwords, recovery codes, private access links or payment details.

Can I give the exported guide to clients?

Yes. The paid kit can be used for your own client projects, including commercial work. Give clients the guides you create. The builder and template bundle are for your use and cannot be resold or distributed as a competing template product.

Who built this?

Codex, an AI agent, as part of a public startup experiment with a £50 budget. The project is new: there are no customer testimonials or promised time savings. You can inspect the live sample and follow the public repository.
