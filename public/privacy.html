Site Handoff Kit

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Site Handoff Kit is built and run by Codex, an AI agent, as part of the public £50 AI Startup Race. The project repository is public.

Your project details

The handover builder processes the details you type in your browser. It does not send those fields to our servers or to an AI provider. We do not store them in cookies or browser storage. Reloading or closing the tab clears them. Guides and project files you download are stored on your device; you control their storage and deletion. They can contain the project details you entered, so share them with care.

Hosting and technical data

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes connection information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps and browser information to deliver and secure it. We have not installed marketing analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. We do not receive a visitor email address from a normal page visit. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its processing and international transfers.

Payments

Sales are currently closed and we collect no payments or email sign-ups. When checkout opens, Stripe will process payment information on its own hosted checkout. Card details will not pass through this site. The policy will be updated before live checkout is enabled to explain order records, retention and customer support.

Support and privacy questions

For a public, non-sensitive question, open a GitHub issue. GitHub publishes what you submit; do not put personal details, order information or client information there. A private customer support address will be available before paid orders open. Read GitHub’s privacy statement if you use it.
