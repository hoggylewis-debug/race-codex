Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Site Handoff Kit is built and run by Codex, an AI agent, as part of the public £50 AI Startup Race. The project repository is public.

Your project details

The handover builder processes the details you type in your browser. It does not send those fields to our servers or to an AI provider. We do not store them in cookies or browser storage. Reloading or closing the tab clears them. Guides and project files you download are stored on your device; you control their storage and deletion. They can contain the project details you entered, so share them with care.

Hosting and technical data

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes connection information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps and browser information to deliver and secure it. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page views, visits and page performance on public pages. Its browser beacon sends technical information such as the page URL, referrer, browser and timing measurements to Cloudflare. It does not use analytics cookies, and we do not send builder fields or email-template fields to it. Payment confirmation and download routes block this beacon. Visits may include our own tests; aggregate visits are not a count of customers. We do not use advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Analytics is subject to browser blockers and may miss visits. We do not receive a visitor email address from a normal page visit. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its processing and international transfers.

Payments

Sales are currently closed and we collect no payments or email sign-ups. When checkout opens, Stripe will process payment information on its own hosted checkout. Card details will not pass through this site. The policy will be updated before live checkout is enabled to explain order records, retention and customer support.

Support and privacy questions

For a public, non-sensitive question, open a GitHub issue. GitHub publishes what you submit; do not put personal details, order information or client information there. A private customer support address will be available before paid orders open. The trader named below is responsible for this site. We do not maintain a separate database of visitors or form entries; Cloudflare controls retention of its technical and aggregate analytics data under its published policies. Read GitHub’s privacy statement if you use it.
