What if the site is not ready?

Use a launch-review message first. List what you need the client to review, the agreed review date and remaining items. Send the final handover email when the site and its guide are ready.

What goes in the handover guide?

Include site and platform details, account ownership and renewals, editing instructions, backups and maintenance, source files, outstanding items and support arrangements. Work through the free 40-point handover checklist to check what is still missing.
